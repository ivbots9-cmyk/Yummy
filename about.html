Yummyland Gifts

Our new gift box studio

For years people bought our candy by the pound and wrote to say they were giving it away. So we made it a proper gift: a burgundy box with sealed cups of our favourites and, inside the lid, two photos of the people it is for.

This site is that studio. Our everyday candy — pouches, bulk and party packs — still lives at yummylandcandy.com.

What goes inside

Our candy

Soft real-fruit gummies, smooth milk chocolate and a few classics — the same candy we have sold since 2018.

Every cup is sealed when it is filled, so flavours stay apart and everything arrives fresh. In the builder you can filter by nut-free, gluten-free and dairy-free, and anything with nuts is marked on its card. Our packing room also handles chocolate with nuts, so for a serious allergy check the label in the box before sharing.
