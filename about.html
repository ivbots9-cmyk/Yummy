About Yummyland

We started as a bulk candy shop. People kept asking us to make it a gift — so we built a box worth giving.

Why a box

Sweet moments for a brighter day

A bag of candy is a snack. A box that opens on two photos, six sealed cups and a card in gold foil is a moment someone keeps. Every Yummyland box is filled and closed by hand.

We keep it simple on purpose: one box, done properly, and a handful of collections we are proud of.

What goes inside

Our candy

The core of the range is Albanese — soft, clean-tasting gummies and smooth milk chocolate. Beside it, a few classics we could not leave out.

Every cup is sealed when it is filled, so flavours stay apart and everything arrives fresh. Candy that contains nuts is marked in the builder. Our gummies and hard candy contain no nuts, but share a packing room with chocolate that does — if an allergy is serious, check each manufacturer’s label before you order.
