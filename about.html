Our story

Candy that turns any moment into a party

Yummyland started with one simple idea: candy is better when you choose it yourself and better still when you share it.

Built one box at a time

We packed our first boxes on a kitchen table for friends’ birthdays. People kept asking for their own mix — more sour, fewer mints, double the gummies — so we built a box builder that lets you decide every single piece.

Today our crew packs thousands of boxes for birthdays, offices, weddings and just-because moments across the USA. Every box is still packed by hand, checked by a human and sent out the same day it is made.
