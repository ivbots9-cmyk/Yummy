Good to know

Questions, answered

Shipping

Every box ships free within the USA. We pack within 1–2 business days and you get a tracking email as soon as it leaves. The packing slip has no prices on it, so you can send a gift straight to its recipient.

In hot months we ship chocolate collections early in the week with a cool pack, so nothing sits in a warehouse over a weekend.

Returns

Candy is food, so we cannot take a box back once it has shipped. If anything arrives damaged or wrong, send us a photo within 7 days and we will send a replacement or refund — your choice.

Personalised boxes (with your photos or your card) can be changed until we start packing — write to us as soon as you can.
