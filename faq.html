Shipping & delivery

We ship across the USA. Free standard shipping on orders over $50.

Returns & happiness guarantee

Candy is a food product, so we cannot accept returns of opened boxes. What we can do is make it right: if your box arrives damaged, melted, incomplete or simply not what you expected, message us within 30 days with your order number and a photo.

We will replace the box or refund you — your choice. No forms, no restocking fees, no arguing about who is right. Unopened boxes can be returned within 14 days for a full refund.
